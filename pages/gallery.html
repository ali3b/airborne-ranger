SOLITARY STRIKE

An isometric commando game in Phaser 3: one soldier, three supply pods, twelve missions behind enemy lines. Every sprite, tileset and map was made with the Synty Sprite Generator through its CLI and MCP tools, from Synty POLYGON 3D models.

Missions

A plane drops one commando and three supply pods behind enemy lines. Complete the objective and reach the extraction zone before the timer runs out. Merit earns promotions (PFC to COL) and medals for a roster of six commandos. Each mission area is a long 128×256 m stretch: drop in the south, objective in the far north.

Supply pods and arsenal

Before each drop, pack the supply pod: carbine magazines, grenades, first-aid kits, LAW rockets and time bombs, up to 21 items and a weight limit. Three pods drop with the commando along the route.

Tilesets

Isometric 64×32 ground tiles with corner-based terrain transitions, shores, cliffs and junction tiles where three or four terrains meet, rasterized by the generator from Synty textures with a climate tint.

How it's made

The game is a test customer for the Synty Sprite Generator. It may only use the generator through its ssg CLI and MCP tools. Every gap it hits is logged as feedback, fixed in the generator, and then re-tested in the game.

SpritesDeclared in ssg.assets.json and built with ssg sprite apply: real scale locked to the 64×32 tile (1 m per tile), isometric 8-direction views, collision footprints and object states.
TilesetsDeclared terrains (grass, dirt, sand, road, water, snow, moss…) with Wang corner sets, exported as Tiled .tsj.
MapsBuilt with MCP edit_map operations and exported as Tiled .tmj with object layers, zones and typed properties.
EnginePhaser 3.80, TypeScript, Vite. Sound is procedural WebAudio.
